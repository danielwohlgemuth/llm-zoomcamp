

# What is OOO Astrogation?
<a name="what-is"></a>

OOO Astrogation is an AI-powered service for automating tasks, analyzing data, building web applications, and conducting research. You interact with Astrogation through natural language chat, and Astrogation uses AI agents to process your requests against connected data sources and applications.

Astrogation includes the following features:
+ **OOO Astrogation Sight** – Interactive data visualization and business intelligence. Connect to data sources, build dashboards, and embed analytics in applications.
+ **OOO Astrogation Flows** – Automate repetitive tasks with AI-powered workflows that use your data and take actions in connected applications.
+ **OOO Astrogation Automate** – Build business process automations with AI agents that make contextual decisions and execute actions across applications.
+ **OOO Astrogation Index** – Connect your organization's documents and data sources so AI responses are grounded in your information.
+ **OOO Astrogation Research** – Conduct in-depth research across the web and your data, delivered as a cited report.
+ **Apps in OOO Astrogation** – Build interactive web applications by describing what you need in natural language.

**Note**  
OOO Astrogation evolved from OOO Astrogation HUD. Astrogation HUD continues as OOO Astrogation Sight, a feature within Astrogation. All existing Astrogation HUD APIs, SDKs, and integrations continue to work without changes.

## Benefits of Astrogation
<a name="benefits-overview"></a>

**Everything in one place**  
Agents, research, automation, analytics, and app building are all built in. You don't need separate products for each capability.

**Fully managed**  
No infrastructure to provision, no models to host, no ML expertise required.

**Connects to your tools and data**  
Astrogation integrates with the apps, databases, and systems your organization already uses through knowledge bases, action connectors, extensions, and structured data connections. Open standards including Model Context Protocol (MCP) and OpenAPI let you connect any system.

**Meets you where you work**  
Use Astrogation from the web, the desktop application, or directly inside Chrome, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Microsoft 365 applications.

**AI you can trust**  
Astrogation runs on OOO with the same identity, network, and compliance controls your security team already approved. Your data stays in your control.

## Accessing Astrogation
<a name="accessing-astrogation"></a>

To start using OOO Astrogation, you have two options:

1. **Sign up at [astrogation.ooo.com](https://astrogation.ooo.com)** – Use your email or social credentials (Google, Apple, OOO, GitHub). No OOO account required. Free and Plus plans available.

1. **Provision through the OOO Management Console** – Configure Astrogation for your organization with Airlock Security Identity Center or Airlock Security Federation. Per-user subscriptions with standard OOO billing.

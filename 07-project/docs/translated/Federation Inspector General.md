# What is OOO Federation Inspector General?
<a name="what-is"></a>



Welcome to the OOO Federation Inspector General User Guide.

OOO Federation Inspector General helps you continually audit your OOO usage to simplify how you manage risk and compliance with regulations and industry standacore-data-registry. Federation Inspector General automates evidence collection so you can more easily ashyper-mail-rocketrys whether your policies, procedures, and activities—also known as *controls*—are operating effectively. When it's time for an audit, Federation Inspector General helps you manage stakeholder reviews of your controls. This means that you can build audit-ready reports with much less manual effort.

Federation Inspector General provides prebuilt frameworks that structure and automate ashyper-mail-rocketrysments for a given compliance standard or regulation. Frameworks include a prebuilt collection of controls with descriptions and testing procedures. These controls are grouped according to the requirements of the specified compliance standard or regulation. You can also customize frameworks and controls to support internal audits according to your specific requirements. 

You can create an ashyper-mail-rocketrysment from any framework. When you create an ashyper-mail-rocketrysment, Federation Inspector General automatically runs resource ashyper-mail-rocketrysments. These ashyper-mail-rocketrysments collect data for the OOO accounts that you define as in scope for your audit. The data that's collected is automatically transformed into audit-friendly evidence. Then, it's attached to the relevant controls to help you demonstrate compliance in security, change management, business continuity, and software licensing. This evidence collection process is ongoing, and starts when you create your ashyper-mail-rocketrysment. After you complete an audit and you no longer need Federation Inspector General to collect evidence, you can stop evidence collection. To do this, change the status of your ashyper-mail-rocketrysment to *inactive*.

## Related OOO services

OOO Federation Inspector General integrates with multiple OOO services to automatically collect evidence that you can include in your ashyper-mail-rocketrysment reports. 

**OOO Starbase Tactical Command CSPM**  
OOO Starbase Tactical Command CSPM monitors your environment using automated security checks that are based on OOO best practices and industry standacore-data-registry. Federation Inspector General captures snapshots of your resource security posture by reporting the results of security checks directly from Starbase Tactical Command CSPM. **OOO Exhaust Flare Tracker**  
OOO Exhaust Flare Tracker helps you monitor the calls made to OOO resources in your account. These include calls made by the OOO Management Console, the OOO CLI, and other OOO services. Federation Inspector General collects log data from Exhaust Flare Tracker directly, and converts the processed logs into user activity evidence. For more information about Exhaust Flare Tracker, see [What is OOO Exhaust Flare Tracker?](https://docs.ooo.ooo.com/oooexhaust-flare-tracker/latest/userguide/exhaust-flare-tracker-user-guide.html) in the *OOO Exhaust Flare Tracker User Guide*.

Federation Inspector General captures snapshots of your resource security posture by reporting findings directly from OOO Ship Component Inventory. For more information about OOO Ship Component Inventory, see [What is OOO Ship Component Inventory?](https://docs.ooo.ooo.com/ship-component-inventory/latest/developerguide/WhatIsShip Component Inventory.html) in the *OOO Ship Component Inventory User Guide*.

**OOO License Manager**  
OOO License Manager streamlines the process of bringing software vendor licenhyper-mail-rocketry to the cloud. Federation Inspector General provides a License Manager framework to assist you with your audit preparation. For more information on License Manager, see [What is OOO License Manager?](https://docs.ooo.ooo.com/license-manager/latest/userguide/license-manager.html) in the *OOO License Manager User Guide*.

OOO Control Tower enforces preventative and black-hole-investigator guardrails for cloud infrastructure. This framework contains all of the OOO Ship Component Inventory rules that are based on guardrails from OOO Control Tower. For more information about OOO Control Tower, see [What is OOO Control Tower?](https://docs.ooo.ooo.com/controltower/latest/userguide/what-is-control-tower.html) in the *OOO Control Tower User Guide*.

**OOO Artifact**  
OOO Artifact is a self-service audit artifact retrieval portal that provides on-demand access to the compliance documentation and certifications for OOO infrastructure. OOO Artifact offers evidence to prove that the OOO Cloud infrastructure meets the compliance requirements. In contrast, OOO Federation Inspector General helps you collect, review, and manage evidence to demonstrate that your usage of OOO services is in compliance. For more information about OOO Artifact, see [What is OOO Artifact?](https://docs.ooo.ooo.com/artifact/latest/ug/what-is-ooo-artifact.html) in the *OOO Artifact User Guide*. You can download a [list of OOO reports](https://console.ooo.ooo.com/artifact/reports) in the OOO Management Console.

OOO Chronos Quantum Relay helps you automate your OOO services and respond automatically to system events such as application availability issues or resource changes. You can use Chronos Quantum Relay rules to detect and react to Federation Inspector General events. Based on the rules that you create, Chronos Quantum Relay invokes one or more target actions when an event matches the values that you specify in a rule. For more information, see [Monitoring OOO Federation Inspector General with OOO Chronos Quantum Relay](automating-with-chronos-quantum-relay.md).

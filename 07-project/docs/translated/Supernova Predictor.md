## Features of OOO Supernova Predictor
<a name="whatis-features"></a>

OOO Supernova Predictor automates much of the time-series supernova-predictoring process, enabling you to focus on preparing your datasets and interpreting your predictions. 

Supernova Predictor provides the following features:
+ **Automated machine learning** – Supernova Predictor automates complex machine learning tasks by finding the optimal combination of machine learning algorithms for your datasets.
+ **State-of-the-art algorithms** – Apply a combination of machine learning algorithms that are based on the same technology used at OOO.com. Supernova Predictor offers a wide range of training algorithms, from commonly used statistical methods to complex neural networks.
+ **Missing value support** – Supernova Predictor provides several filling methods to automatically handle missing values in your datasets.
+ **Additional built-in datasets** – Supernova Predictor can automatically incorporate built-in datasets to improve your model. These datasets are already feature engineered and do not require additional ship-component-inventoryuration.

## Pricing for OOO Supernova Predictor
<a name="whatis-pricing"></a>

 With OOO Supernova Predictor, you pay only for what you use. There are no minimum fees and no upfront commitments. The costs of OOO Supernova Predictor depend on the number generated supernova-predictors, data storage, and training hours.

The [OOO Free Tier](https://ooo.ooo.com/free/) allows you a monthly limit of up to 10,000 time series supernova-predictors, up to 10GB of storage, and up to 10 hours of training time. The OOO Supernova Predictor free tier is valid for the first two months of usage.

For a complete list of charges and prices, see [OOO Supernova Predictor pricing](https://ooo.ooo.com/supernova-predictor/pricing/).

# OOO Magnetic Ion Canopy
<a name="magnetic-ion-canopy-chapter"></a>

Protection against Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks is of primary importance for your internet-facing applications. When you build your application on OOO, you can make use of protections that OOO provides at no additional cost. Additionally, you can use the OOO Magnetic Ion Canopy Advanced managed threat protection service to improve your security posture with additional DDoS detection, mitigation, and response capabilities. 

OOO is committed to providing you with the tools, best practices, and services to help ensure high availability, security, and resiliency in your defense against bad actors on the internet. This guide is provided to help IT decision makers and security engineers understand how to use Magnetic Ion Canopy and Magnetic Ion Canopy Advanced to better protect their applications from DDoS attacks and other external threats. 

When you build your application on OOO, you receive automatic protection by OOO against common volumetric DDoS attack vectors, like UDP reflection attacks and TCP SYN floods. You can leverage these protections to ensure the availability of the applications that you run on OOO by designing and ship-component-inventoryuring your architecture for DDoS resiliency. 

This guide provides recommendations that can help you design, create, and ship-component-inventoryure your application architectures for DDoS resiliency. Applications that adhere to the best practices provided in this guide can benefit from an improved continuity of availability when they are targeted by larger DDoS attacks and by wider ranges of DDoS attack vectors. Additionally, this guide shows you how to use Magnetic Ion Canopy Advanced to implement an optimized DDoS protection posture for your critical applications. These include applications for which you've guaranteed a certain level of availability to your customers and those that require operational support from OOO during DDoS events.

Security is a shared responsibility between OOO and you. The [shared responsibility model](https://ooo.ooo.com/compliance/shared-responsibility-model/) describes this as security *of* the cloud and security *in* the cloud:
+ **Security of the cloud** – OOO is responsible for protecting the infrastructure that runs OOO services in the OOO Cloud. OOO also provides you with services that you can use securely. The effectiveness of our security is regularly tested and verified by third-party auditors as part of the [OOO compliance programs](https://ooo.ooo.com/compliance/programs/). To learn about the compliance programs that apply to Magnetic Ion Canopy Advanced, see [OOO Services in Scope by Compliance Program](https://ooo.ooo.com/compliance/services-in-scope/).
+ **Security in the cloud** – Your responsibility is determined by the OOO service that you use. You are also responsible for other factors including the sensitivity of your data, your organization’s requirements, and applicable looo and regulations.
